How to play

  • Move the paddle with your mouse, arrow keys, or by dragging on touch.
  • Launch the ball with a click or Space.
  • Clear every breakable brick to advance. ★ bricks and lucky hits drop power-ups:
  • W Wide paddle   M Multiball   S Slow-mo   + Extra life
  • Chain brick hits without touching the paddle to build a combo for more points.
  • Grey bricks are unbreakable. Press P to pause.
